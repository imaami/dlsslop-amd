dlsslop-amd third-party notices
===============================

DLSS Linux Open Proxy for AMD integrates and adapts these components:

* DLSS5VKLayer by bmitch87 and contributors, including Thomas Eric: Vulkan
  presentation layer and shared-memory protocol (AGPL-3.0.txt).
  https://github.com/bmitch87/DLSS5VKLayer
* AMD neural scheduler and HIP kernels by Kien / lmxxf (MIT-amd.txt).
  https://github.com/lmxxf/dlss5-on-amd-9070xt-porting
* OptiScaler shader lineage and Dagherbou/OptiScaler_DLSSNR snapshot
  97376162 (GPL-3.0.txt). The current integration is a modified derivative.
  https://github.com/optiscaler/OptiScaler
  https://github.com/Dagherbou/OptiScaler_DLSSNR
* RenoDX color-composition design by clshortfuse / Carlos Lopez Jr.,
  reimplemented through OptiScaler (RenoDX.txt contains attribution and MIT terms).
  https://github.com/clshortfuse/renodx
* Matched residual and cube scaling technique by hhkbble; native + edit
  enlargement technique by xen/xenmods (DLSSNR-Cost-Scaler, MIT, no copied code).
  https://github.com/xenmods/DLSSNR-Cost-Scaler
* New independent integration code (MIT-integration.txt); copied or adapted
  material retains the licenses and notices of its authors.

Exact release sources and dependency pins are identified in SOURCES.
